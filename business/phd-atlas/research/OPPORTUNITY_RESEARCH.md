# Opportunity research and selection

Prepared 2026-10-21. Labels: **[F]** verified fact (source linked, checked on this date), **[E]** estimate or assumption, **[N]** taken from your Notion Second Brain.

## Constraints that shaped the search

- [N] You are a full-time McMaster Physics PhD student and TA on a study permit (24 h/week off-campus allowance reported), with a BS-MS from IISER Thiruvananthapuram.
- [N] Three earlier business attempts produced zero sales: Small Hour Mysteries (Etsy), Spectroscopy Funding Brief, and SpecReply. They stalled at the step of getting a first buyer, because B2B outreach needed approvals and the products were built before demand was shown.
- [F] IRCC: "If you're a self-employed person, you're also responsible for keeping track of the hours you work off campus." The limit is 24 h/week during terms and unlimited during scheduled breaks. [IRCC: work off campus](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/work-off-campus.html). In May 2026 IRCC clarified that hours are "any time they spend earning wages or collecting a commission". [Moving2Canada, 2026-05-27](https://moving2canada.com/news/gig-work-hours-international-students-may-2026/)
- [E] The safest reading is that every business hour counts toward the 24-hour cap, together with any SpecReply hours. A business needing ≤3 h/week fits well inside it.

## How competition was assessed

1. I counted live listings in each niche on the marketplace where buyers actually shop (fetched live on 2026-10-21).
2. I used review counts as a **lower bound** on purchases, since only a fraction of buyers leave reviews.
3. I checked whether existing products match the current curriculum or cycle (AP Physics 1 was redesigned for the May 2025 exam; the TOEFL scale changed on Jan 21, 2026).
4. I looked for incumbents that are hard to beat: brands, credentialed sellers, venture-funded platforms.
5. I checked each platform's rules on AI-built products, because I (an AI) would produce most of the assets.
6. Etsy and Amazon block automated fetching, so evidence there comes through search engines and is weaker.

## The five opportunities compared

### 1. Physics "case file" mystery review games for high-school / AP Physics 1 teachers (TPT)
- **Demand [F]:** one seller's 20 physics "Whodunnit" games ($6 each) carry about 618 reviews combined (individual games at 123, 96, 79, 64 and 59). A full-year AP Physics 1 slide course at $259.99 has 90 reviews. [TPT search: physics whodunnit](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/browse?search=physics%20whodunnit)
- **Gap [F]:** that seller's games are conceptual and middle-school level. "AP physics review game" returns only Jeopardy decks, almost none with reviews.
- **Market [F]:** 174,992 students took AP Physics 1 in May 2025 ([College Board](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap25-physics-1-score-distributions.pdf)). About 27,600 US teachers teach physics ([AIP](https://www.aip.org/statistics/high-school-physics-overview)).
- **First 10 customers:** TPT's own search traffic; no audience needed.
- **Cost:** $29 once.
- **Margins:** 55% payout on Basic, 77.5% on Exclusive. Support is low.
- **Blocker [F]:** TPT's AI ban means the problems, solutions, story and listing text must be written by you, at an estimated [E] 2–2.5 h per game.
- **Verdict:** best evidence of the five. **Declined by you on 2026-10-21** because it needs your authorship.

### 2. AP Physics 1 (2025-format) free-response workbook (Amazon KDP + Payhip)
- **Demand [F]:** 174,992 test-takers. Barron's 2026 edition lists at $29.99. Fiveable sells unit exams at $5 and a full exam at $12 ([Fiveable](https://fiveable.me/shop/ap-physics-1-revised-full-length-exam.md)).
- **Competition [F]:** a self-published "AP Physics 1 Prep Book 2026-2027" Kindle book (Sept 2026) already exists. One TPT seller has a 152-FRQ bundle. The gap is weak.
- **Fit [E]:** most hands-off operationally (Amazon prints, delivers and supports), but discovery for a new title is slow without ads, sales peak in Mar–May, and you would spend 8–12 h checking the physics once.

### 3. International physics-PhD application kit with a verified program database (Gumroad), **chosen**
- **Demand [F]:**
  - 177,892 graduate students from India were in the US in 2024/25 ([Open Doors India fact sheet](https://opendoorsdata.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/OD_India_Fact-Sheet-2025.pdf)).
  - SOP reviews sell for $50–200 on Fiverr ([example](https://fiverr.com/phd_po_editor/revise-phd-application-materials-personal-statement-cv)).
  - Generic Gumroad application kits sell for $1–£39.99 ([phdtoprof](https://phdtoprof.gumroad.com/l/phd-application), [skatergrandpa](https://skatergrandpa.gumroad.com/l/pzdlf)).
- **Competition [F]:** AIP's free GradSchoolShopper lists department-supplied profiles but no international-specific fields such as fee waivers for internationals, English-test waivers for Indian degrees, or Canada's supervisor-first rule ([AIP](https://students.aip.org/observer/grad-school-shopper-com-simplifies-the-grad-school-search)). There is also plenty of free advice on Reddit and GradCafe.
- **Gap [F, from our own data collection]:** the rules really are scattered and sometimes contradictory. Toronto's own pages disagree on its fee ($135 vs $130). UT Austin's physics page and university page disagree on Duolingo. Several top programs now require the Physics GRE again. Alberta's English-exemption list covers the IITs but not the IISERs.
- **First 10 customers:** your IISER/IIT network plus Reddit. The season is open now (deadlines Dec 1–Jan 15).
- **Headwinds [F]:** the international doctoral applicant pool fell 21% for 2026-27, and 41% of physics/astronomy departments reported fewer international applications ([AIP](https://www.aip.org/statistics/federal-funding-and-visa-uncertainty-shape-fall-2026-graduate-admissions-in-physics-and-astronomy)).
- **Fit [E]:** I can produce everything. Ongoing work is about 1 h/week in season. The ceiling is small and sales are seasonal.

### 4. Homeschool high-school physics, self-grading course
- **Demand [F]:**
  - Derek Owens' online physics runs about $65/month plus a $15 registration fee ([review](https://homeschoolstartguide.com/blog/derek-owens-homeschool-review)).
  - BookShark's physics kit costs $549.99, and a live class costs $940/yr.
  - NHERI estimates about 3.4M US homeschoolers (via a secondary source).
- **Fit [E]:** high willingness to pay, but a heavy build. Acquisition needs you active in forums, and parents send support questions. It doesn't fit 2–3 h/week.

### 5. Physics-teacher micro-SaaS (randomized, self-grading problem sets)
- **Demand [F]:** paid incumbents exist (DeltaMath, Positive Physics, WebAssign; prices mostly by quote), plus free WeBWorK and MyOpenMath.
- **Fit [E]:** the gap is weak, there is no marketplace to bring buyers, the first sale would take months, and student-data privacy and support make it heavy.

### Screened out early
- Tes physics escape rooms: £2–4 and mostly 0 reviews ([shop](https://www.tes.com/teaching-resources/shop/Cre8tive_Resources/Physics)).
- Boom Cards: buyers skew K-8.
- Etsy printables: flooded with AI products, and Small Hour Mysteries is already on hold.
- Notion AI-memory templates: Notion now has built-in Memory.
- Canadian bookkeeping sheets: a credentialed bookkeeper already sells them ([FluentBook](https://www.etsy.com/shop/FluentBook)).
- Shopify apps: no domain advantage and heavy support.
- SOP-editing service: trades your hours directly for money.

## Recommendation and tradeoffs (after your choice)

**Selected: Physics PhD Atlas 2027 (Option 3).** It is the best option compatible with AI-built assets, and its first ten customers are reachable through your own network during the current season. Once launched, it needs very little of your time.

Accepted tradeoffs:
1. **Small ceiling:** the base case is under ~$1k net per season (see `financials.py`).
2. **Seasonality:** sales concentrate in Sep–Jan.
3. **Low willingness to pay:** most buyers are price-sensitive, so the plan uses Gumroad's regional pricing.
4. **Late start this cycle:** many applicants have already built their lists.
5. **Platform dependence:** everything runs through Gumroad.

The upside path is annual editions, adjacent disciplines (chemistry, mathematics), and a free SEO site next season. None of it should be built until the validation gates pass.
