# Automated and template emails

Set the first two up once as Gumroad **Workflows** (Emails → Workflows → "Purchase" trigger). They then run with no involvement from you.

## Workflow 1: Welcome (send immediately after purchase)

**Subject:** Your Physics PhD Atlas: start here (2 minutes)

Hi {first name or "there"},

Thanks for getting the Physics PhD Atlas 2027.

1. Unzip the download and open **Atlas.html** in any browser (it works offline).
2. Set the *Physics GRE* and *English-test waiver* filters to match you, then star 8–15 programs.
3. Read Playbook Sections 2–7 this week. They cover the decisions with deadlines this month, including Europe.

Before paying any application fee, tap the value in the Atlas and open the official page. Rules can change mid-cycle.

If you spot something changed or wrong, reply here with the program and page link.

Good luck,
{your first name}

## Workflow 2: Usefulness check (send 10 days after purchase)

**Subject:** One quick question about the Atlas

Hi,

One question, and a one-line reply is perfect:

**What, if anything, did the Atlas change in your applications?** (For example: a program added or dropped, a fee waiver requested, a test booked or skipped, a supervisor emailed.)

And one yes/no: **did checkout work on your first try?**

Your answers decide what goes into the next update. If it wasn't useful, tell me and I'll refund you; you have 14 days.

Thanks,
{your first name}
