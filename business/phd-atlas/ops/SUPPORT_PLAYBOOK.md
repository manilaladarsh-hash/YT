# Support playbook (target: under 30 min/week)

| Message type | What to do | Template |
|---|---|---|
| Refund request | Approve in Gumroad, no questions asked, within 14 days | `store/EMAILS.md` → refund reply |
| "A value is wrong/changed" | Thank them, then forward the program and link to a Claude session for verification. A confirmed fix ships in the next update | `store/EMAILS.md` → data error reply |
| "Card declined" | Suggest enabling international online transactions in their banking app, or paying with PayPal | FAQ in `store/LISTING_COPY.md` |
| "Can't open the files" | Atlas.html opens in any browser; Atlas.xlsx works in Google Sheets via File → Import; unzip first on phones | n/a |
| "Will I get admitted?" / profile evaluation | Politely decline: out of scope for the kit | "That's beyond what the Atlas can tell you. A professor or current grad student in your field is the best person to ask." |
| Visa / study permit questions | Decline and point to official sources | "Please check travel.state.gov (US) or canada.ca/IRCC (Canada). I can't advise on immigration." |
| Abuse, piracy reports | Ignore unless large-scale; never threaten buyers | n/a |
