Stipend and Support
Stipend and support for the most talented research elites
A generous package for recipients of:
HKU Presidential PhD Scholar Programme (HKU-PS)
• Applicable to selected full-time PhD entrants
• Scholarship Package up to around HK$1.7M
Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPF)*
*The package for recipients of HKPF will be automatically upgraded to an
HKU-PS one.
Cash Award to support research & living expenses
HK$40,000 in Year 1; HK$20,000/year for the
remaining normative study period
Conference & Research-related Travel Allowance
of HK$14,400/year
Postgraduate Scholarship (PGS)
of HK$28,700/month
Waiver of Tuition Fees
for the whole normative study period
Guaranteed Accommodation in Year 1
Hall place guaranteed in Year 1, with possibility of renewal
For non HKU-PS / non-HKPF Research Postgraduate (RPg) students
Postgraduate Scholarship (PGS)
- Applicable to full-time MPhil and PhD applicants
- Funding value: HK$19,135/month or HK$19,655/month# (wef 1/9/2026)
Tuition Waiver Scheme
(for Local RPg Students)
#for PhD students whose candidatures have been confirmed

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD)
Doctor of Philosophy (PhD)
The University offers 3-year and 4-year PhD programmes. Applicants who have a good Bachelor’s degree with honours and/or a taught Master’s degree will be considered for admission to a 4-year PhD programme, whereas those who already hold a research Master’s degree (e.g. MPhil) will only be considered for admission to a 3-year PhD programme.
PhD programmes are offered in full-time and part-time modes and the periods of study are as follows:
Year
Mode
Normative Study Period
Probationary Period
3-year
Full-time
36 months
12 months
Part-time
54 months
18 months
4-year
Full-time
48 months
18 months
Part-time
72 months
24 months
Candidates for 4-year PhD programme will be required to follow an approved course of study and research, including coursework on discipline-specific knowledge, language and research skills. The coursework component is well designed to equip students for their research work and thesis writing. Candidates for 3-year PhD programme may also be required to take courses and satisfy coursework requirements. Please click here for details of the coursework requirements.
A successful PhD thesis should represent the result of the candidate's research which should be an original contribution to knowledge and worthy of publication.

Research Groups
Besides commitment to excellence in undergraduate education, the staff of the Physics Department are engaged in active research in many areas of physics. The department offers both M.Phil. and Ph.D. programs for full-time postgraduate students.
Five Major Areas of Researches
Astronomy and Astrophysics Group
Atomic, Optical and Quantum Physics Group
Experimental Condensed Matter and Material Science Group
Theoretical and Computational Condensed Matter Group
Experimental Nuclear and Particle Physics Group

A Nexus for Next-Generation BME
SBME is a trailblazer in redefining BME education and research — empowering the next generation to transform healthcare by linking engineering, life sciences, and clinical translation.
The School of Biomedical Engineering (SBME) was founded in March 2025 and represents a unique collaboration between the Faculties of Medicine, Engineering, Dentistry and Sciences at the University of Hong Kong.

Design Principles for π-conjugated Hydrogel Semiconductors
Professor Shiming Zhang

The semiconducting behaviour in hydrogels enables advanced electronic applications for biosystems. This Review provides key principles of the design, fabrication, characterizations and benchmarking for π-conjugated Hydrogel Semiconductors.

Degree Programme
Our Biomedical Engineering (BEng (BME)) programme is training a new generation of biomedical engineers to transform healthcare by combining a deep understanding of human biology with innovative engineering solutions. We empower students to apply fundamental principles and quantitative analysis to address urgent biomedical problems across the lifespan, to enhance health and quality of life and promote healthy aging.
