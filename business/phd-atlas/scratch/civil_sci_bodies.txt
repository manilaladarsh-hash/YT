INTERNATIONAL GRADUATE PROGRAM
The Department of Civil Engineering at the University of Tokyo boasts one of the most advanced and progressive global academic environments in the world, and has admission systems to recruit talented students internationally. In order to reach the global talent pool, the Department of Civil Engineering recruits students through both international admission and regular admission. International students living outside Japan can apply for international admission where applicants do not need to travel to Tokyo to take entrance exams. These international students can alternatively choose to take entrance exams for regular admission. On the other hand, international students already in Japan can apply only for regular admission and must take the university’s entrance exams.
Prospective students who wish to obtain international admission and study at the Department of Civil Engineering must obtain a full-scholarship as shown in the figure below, which also provides details of the three categories of scholarship available:

ELIGIBILITY
Applicants must satisfy all of the eligibility requirements for the International Graduate Program of the Department of Civil Engineering as mentioned below in Articles (1.) to (3.). In addition, applicants must also fulfil one of the eligibility requirements described in Articles (4.) and (5.).
1.A foreign national who is not a permanent resident of Japan.
2.A foreign national who is not currently residing in Japan.
3.Individuals with the linguistic skills required to take classes and receive research guidance in English.
Master’s course applicants
4.Those who have completed, or are expected to complete, 16 years of school education by September 30 of the enrolment year, upon completion of their Bachelor's or equivalent degree.*
Doctoral course applicants
5.Those who have completed, or are expected to complete, 18 years of school education by September 30 of the enrolment year, upon completion of their Master's or equivalent degree.*
* Certified proof must be provided if the educational curriculum in the applicant's country requires less than 16 years for completion of a Bachelor's degree, or less than 18 years for completion of a Master's degree, or their equivalents.

The Graduate School does not authorize any individuals or organizations to provide paid admissions or application support on its behalf.
Accordingly, the Graduate School assumes no responsibility for any problems, disputes, or disadvantages that may arise from the use of such unauthorized services.
Please ensure that all application preparations are made strictly in accordance with the guidelines published on the official Graduate School website. Please also be aware that some unauthorized agents may be involved in fraudulent or illegal practices. (December 26, 2025)

When these documents become available for download, a "printable notification" email will be sent from the Online Application Site (e-apply). Please log in to your My Page and download them yourself. For the specific download periods, please check the Application Guidelines. ※ Only eligible applicants can download these documents from their My Page.
・If you have any questions, please check "6. FAQs" at the bottom of this page.
