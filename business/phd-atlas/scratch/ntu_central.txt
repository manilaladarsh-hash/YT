NTU Research Scholarship
The NTU Research Scholarship is awarded to outstanding graduate students for research.
You are eligible for this scholarship if you are a local or international student seeking admission as a full-time candidate pursuing a Graduate Research programme at NTU.
You must have a minimum of Honours (Distinction) or its equivalent.
There is no bond attached to the scholarship.
The award is tenable for one (1) year in the first instance and is renewable subject to good progress. The maximum period of the Scholarship is two (2) years for Master's candidates and four (4) years for PhD candidates, as determined by school as well as availability of research funding in each case.

Provost Graduate Award
The Provost Graduate Award (PGA) is a distinguished award presented by the NTU Provost to PhD students demonstrating outstanding academic achievements and research potential in their field of study.
All nationalities are eligible for the scholarship.
An Honours (Highest Distinction) or equivalent at Bachelor's level.
Candidates who have not yet completed their undergraduate degree must provide official documentation from their university confirming they are on track to graduate with an Honours (Highest Distinction) degree or equivalent.
There is no bond attached to the scholarship.
The award is tenable for one year in the first instance and is renewable subject to good progress. The maximum period of the Award covers 4 years of the PhD candidature.

Frequently Asked Questions
I am unable to submit my application due to an error message. What can I do?
This may be due to your application form being incomplete. Please ensure that you have filled in all mandatory fields in the application form. Mandatory fields are marked with an asterisk (*)
Please also check that you haveΓÇ»completed the Criminal Records Declaration section that can be found on the "Programme Enrolled in NTU/Declaration" page.
I am unable to rank my program choices during the application stage. What should I do?
Ranking is compulsory in the application portal. You will not be able to rank your application choices only if you have already received an outcome from NTU for your application. Your applications will be assessed separately by the different schools. If you are selected by both schools, you may receive multiple offers and may proceed to accept 1 programme as your final choice.
I missed uploading some crucial documents during the application stage. What should I do?
You may go to the application portal and select "AFTER Submission of Online Application", then select "Upload any other documents".
When will I receive my offer letter?
The period when application outcomes are released vary for different programmes. You may check your application status by clicking 'Check Status'.
How do I accept my offer?
To accept your offer of admission, please click this link.
Can my application results be expedited?
The university handles a large volume of applications, each carefully reviewed. We aim to provide the outcomes as soon as they are available. To check the status of your application, please visit this link.
How do I apply?
You can submit an application online at NTU Graduate Online Application.
I have submitted my online application, but I cannot find the payment link.
Please make payment at the same link you used to apply: NTU Graduate Online Application.
I do not have a passport. Can I still apply?
International students are required to provide their passport number. If you do not possess a passport, you may alternatively provide your national identification number.
How can I apply for both CCDS and MSE programmes, and can I select them as my 1st and 2nd choices on the application form?
You would need to submit two separate online applications. You can apply at NTU Graduate Online Application.
How can I track the status of my application?
Please check your application status here.
How do I accept the offer?
To accept your NTU offer, please click on the acceptance link provided within your offer letter. Please ensure you respond by the deadline specified in the email.
Can referees send hardcopy of their reports to the University?
Please do not ask your referee to send a hard copy to the university. Our application process is now entirely paperless. Referees may email the respective school directly for late submissions.
How do I send my ETS test scores to NTU?
Please upload your results to your application.
My referees do not have institutional Email ID.
We strongly encourage applicants to submit a letter of recommendation from a referee with an official institutional email address. Alternatively, we will also accept letters from employers or individuals with an academic background who can attest to your suitability for the programme
How to get exemption from GRE & TOEFL/IETLS test scores?
Test score (TOEFL/IELTS, GRE) exemptions are granted on a case-by-case basis at the School's discretion. Contact the School directly for more information.
What internet browsers are recommended for submitting my application?
It is strongly recommended to use Chrome/Edge or above when accessing the online application portal.
Can I use mobile devices such as tablets and smart phones when applying?
It is strongly recommended to use a computer or laptop when accessing the online application portal.
Do I need to submit a separate application for scholarship?
You are not required to submit a separate application form for scholarship. Please indicate if you wish to be considered for scholarship in the application form. Your application will be reviewed by the school accordingly.

Research Programmes Admission Guide
Students are required to pursue an independent but supervised research on an approved topic based on which a thesis must be submitted for examination. Students are also required to attend classes and pass the examinations.

We welcome graduates with strong academic potential and a passion for research to apply for NTUΓÇÖs postgraduate research programmes. To support a holistic and evidence-based evaluation, applicants are required to submit the following documents and demonstrate the following competencies:
Academic Qualifications
BachelorΓÇÖs Degree: A strong BachelorΓÇÖs degree with minimum Honours (Distinction) and its equivalent.
MasterΓÇÖs Degree (where applicable)
English Language Proficiency: A good TOEFL/IELTS score for applicants whose BachelorΓÇÖs degree is not conducted in English.
-Validity: Test scores must be within 2 years from the date of application.
Research Potential and Motivation
Research Proposal (where applicable): A well-developed proposal that reflects research interests, potential, and motivation.
Resume: A resume that highlights personal and academic achievements, prior research experience and skills, leadership and character.
Personal Statement: A narrative outlining the applicantΓÇÖs academic journey, research interests, and goals.
Abstracts of Research Publications (where applicable)
Academic References: Two academic references providing insights into the applicantΓÇÖs academic capabilities and character.
Test Scores (where applicable)
GRE / GMAT / GATE are optional*.
- Strong evidence of quantitative, verbal, and/or analytical skills through these tests will enhance your application.
Validity
GRE / GMAT: Within 5 years from the date of application;
GATE (for applicants from India): Minimum 90th percentile, within 3 years from the date of application
*Some programmes may still require these test scores. Please refer to the admission requirement of the respective programme here.
Video Essay (for programmes under the College of Engineering, College of Science, and Interdisciplinary Graduate Programme): Applicants are required to submit a short video presentation as part of their application.

Nanyang President's Graduate Scholarship
The Nanyang President's Graduate Scholarship (NPGS) is a competitive and prestigious scholarship scheme designed to encourage outstanding graduates or final-year students to take their first step towards a leading research career by studying for a full time PhD at NTU. The premier scholarship granted by NTU's President, recognizing PhD students who exemplify exceptional academic, research and leadership qualities.
All nationalities are eligible for the scholarship.
An Honours (Highest Distinction) or equivalent at Bachelor's level.
Candidates who have not yet completed their undergraduate degree must provide official documentation from their university confirming they are on track to graduate with an Honours (Highest Distinction) degree or equivalent.
There is no bond attached to the scholarship.
The award is tenable for one year in the first instance and is renewable subject to good progress. The maximum period of the Scholarship is 4 years for PhD candidates, as determined by school as well as availability of research funding in each case.
