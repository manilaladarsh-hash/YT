Education and Research Goals
The education and research goals at this graduate school are to develop talented individuals who, with a
basis in engineering, boldly take up the challenge of exploring unexplored fields and new technological
innovations, contribute to the sustainability and development of human society, and who are able to acquire
systematic knowledge regarding science and technology as well as an engineering -based thinking, and have
the ability to solve social problems with an understanding of diversity and a broad perspe ctive.
Admission Policy
1. Desired student profile
1) Strongly motivated to study in accordance with the educational research goals of the Graduate School.
2) In addition to advanced expertise in one’s major field of study, possesses engineering and logical
thinking skills as well as creativity, and has a strong desire to solve social problems.
3) Possesses excellent communication skills, foreign language proficiencies, and sound ethics, respects
diversity, and aspires to contribute to the international community.
2. Selection Policy
Applicants are selected through written and oral examinations, and submitted documents are evaluated
based on the desired student profile. Academic records and foreign language skills are taken into
consideration as well. In this selection process, empha sis will be placed on the following.
・High level of expertise in field of interest, and deep engineering -based thinking skills.
・Ability to discover unknown problems and develop creative solutions.
・International communication skills and ability to disseminate research results through those skills.

AY 2027 Graduate School of Engineering Entrance Examinations
Guidelines for Submission of TOEFL Scores
Official TOEFL iBT or TOEFL iBT Home Edition scores must be submitted as specified below.
〔About TOEFL scores〕
・The Graduate School of Engineering accepts “Test Date Scores” only. “MyBest scores” are not accepted.
・Applicants should check the ETS website for information on how to apply, required equipment and examination environment
of the TOEFL iBT Home Editon on their own.
・Applicants cannot submit score sheets from group testing systems such as TOEFL ITP.
・TOEFL ITP will not be conducted on the day of Graduate School of Engineering entrance examination.
・Applicants should submit TOEFL scores even if their native language is English.
〔How to submit TOEFL scores〕
Please complete all three procedures listed below (①, ② and ③) for the submission of your scores.
①Obtain a TOEFL iBT score and send your official TOEFL scores
When registering for a TOEFL test, be sure to request ETS to transmit your scores according to “Send TOEFL Institutional
Score Report to” below through your ETS account, by the deadline of the department you wish to apply for. If you did not
request ETS to transmit your scores at the time of registration, please do so as soon as possible. If you have successfully
requested the official TOEFL scores transmission and there is no problem during your TOEFL test, we typically receive your
score report data online about 5 days (from Jan.21,2026 onward) after you take the test. The scores will be considered
officially received only when its data is verifiable by this graduate school.
Send TOEFL Institutional Score Report to:
DI (Designated Institution) Code: "8596"
Department Code: "99"
・However, test rescheduling may occur due to network or equipment problems, and delays in issuing official scores
may arise due to various other reasons. Please plan ahead to allow ample time for taking the test.
・It is possible to transmit your official TOEFL scores before your application to this graduate school. If a valid score report
was previously sent through “Send TOEFL Institutional Score Report to” above, re-submission is not required.
・We do not accept individual inquiries about the arrival of scores.
②Register your Appointment Number and the Test Date on the WEB Application System (My Page).
Register your Appointment Number and the test date on My Page of the WEB Application System by the deadline of the
department you wish to apply for.
・You may send your scores multiple times. However, we will only accept the score report that corresponds to the
Appointment Number and the test date registered on the WEB Application System.
③Upload your Test Taker Score Report to the WEB Application System (My Page).
Upload your Test Taker Score Report (PDF) that corresponds to the Appointment Number and test date you registered on the
WEB Application System by the deadline set by the department you wish to apply for. You will be able to download your Test
Taker Score Report (PDF) from your ETS account starting around 72 hours (from Jan.21,2026 onward) after your test date.
・The Appointment Number, Test Date and Test Taker Score Report can be modified on the Web Application System as
many times as necessary, as long as it is within the score submission deadline of the department you wish to apply for.
〔TOEFL Scores Validity Period〕
・Master’s Program, Doctoral Program【Application schedule A】 Scores valid for test dates after September 2024.
・Master’s Program【Sch.B, if offered】, Doctoral Program【Sch.B】 Scores valid for test dates after February 2025.

The Department of Materials Engineering is recruiting 45 master’s students and 20 doctoral students. In response to the expansion of the field of materials engineering in recent years, we welcome the applicants who not only have been educated in materials engineering, but also have learned the basics of materials engineering such as physics and chemistry, and who are willing to learn materials engineering from the standpoint of applying it to bio / mechanical / electronic devices. Please refer to the Graduate School of Engineering website (https://www.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/soe/admission/general-number) for the number of applicants and successful applicants last year.
Examination Content
[Master’s course]
The Entrance Examination is based on the Foreign Language (English) Examination by submitting the official score of TOEFL or TOEIC L&R , the written examination (general education subjects and fundamentals of materials), and the Oral Examination. The Entrance Examination for the master’s program is conducted with the same subjects for all examinees. Please refer to the Guide to Entrance Examination for details.
[Doctoral course]
The Entrance Examination is based on the Foreign Language (English) Examination by submitting the official score of TOEFL or TOEIC L&R , the Written Examination (fundamentals of materials), and the Oral Examination. However, for those who have completed or are expected to complete the master’s program in the Department of Mining and Metallurgy, Department of Metallurgical Engineering, Department of Materials Science, and Department of Materials Engineering of Graduate School of Engineering, the University of Tokyo, the Foreign Language (English) Examination and the Written Examination will be omitted. For those who have completed or are expected to complete the master’s program at the other departments of Graduate School of Engineering, the University of Tokyo, the Foreign Language (English) will be omitted. The problems in the Written Examination are the same as those for the Master’s course.
In the Environment Management Engineering Course, we are recruiting company employees who wish to be enrolled on the theme of environmental management and related research. (Refer to the attached table at the end of the Guide to Entrance Examination (PDF))
Applicants for the Doctoral program should contact the possible supervisor of interest in advance and gain approval for the supervision by the time the application is submitted, and explain the reason for the application upon the oral examination.
Please refer to the Guide to Entrance Examination for details.

Admission
The Graduate Council of the University of Tokyo grants admission to this program based upon the recommendation of the Graduate School of Engineering. Screening for admission is based both on the evaluation of documents submitted by the international students and, if necessary, on the internet interviews and examinations conducted by the faculty. There are three application periods (July application for April Enrollment, September application and January application for October Enrollment). When the evaluation process is complete, applicants who are evaluated as qualified and meet the IME criteria will be granted admission to this program. At September Application, top qualified applicants among them will be nominated as candidates for scholarships provided by IME Graduate Program.

Department Overview
The mission of Department of Computer Science is to contribute to research and education on
fundamental theories of computer science, their implementations as computer systems, and
applications to natural science, which provide foundations for information technologies
indispensable in today’s society. Of particular interest is in-depth research on the core of
information and computation rather than the use of information technologies as a tool. The
research focus includes, but is not limited to: Discrete Mathematics, Numerical Algorithms,
Programming Languages, Algorithms, Quantum Computation, Computer Architecture,
Operating Systems, Software, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Bioinformatics,
Computational Science, Natural Language Processing, Information Retrieval, Computer
Vision, Human Computer Interaction, and Graphics. For more details, refer to the document
“Faculty and Labs in Department of Computer Science” provided separately.

Scholarships
The University of Tokyo provides international students with access to a number of financial aid options that cover various forms and purposes, including school expense exemptions, scholarships, emergency loans, and student discounts.
The following are the scholarships available at the Graduate School of Science. If you have further questions about the scholarships, please contact the International Team in the Academic Affairs Group.

By Embassy Recommendation
Contact Office:Embassy or Consulate of Japan in your home country

The Embassy or Consulate of Japan in your home country is responsible for this scholarship, and there are different requirements and policies according to each country. to the Global Science Graduate Course (GSGC)

Educational and Research Objectives
The mission of education and research in science is to create new streams of knowledge, develop them, and pass
them on to future generations, in never-ending search for a fundamental understanding of nature. The Graduate
School of Science, the University of Tokyo, seeks to build, upon the foundation of its prominence in research, a hub
for the mobilization of intellect on a global scale. This is where the next generation of researchers absorbs the
principles and methodologies of science, and in doing so evolves into global citizens equipped with the alacrity,
ingenuity, and scientific resources required to tackle heretofore unknown challenges in the future world. Involving a
greater number of international students to globalize the learning environment in which cutting edge education and
research advance hand in hand is essential for this goal. The Global Science Graduate Course (GSGC) has been
established to help create such an environment. The GSGC allows its students to obtain their degrees using English
only, provides them with financial support in the form of a scholarship, and assists them under the framework of its
industry-government-academia cooperation as they seek employment after obtaining their degrees. In doing so, the
program aims to attract excellent university graduates from universities all over the world, and foster world-class
science professionals, by standardizing a five-year integrated education scheme in which students attend both the
Master’s course and the Doctoral course in sequence.
What We Look for in GSGC Applicants
• Those who are capable of grasping phenomena objectively with a focus on a universal view point, developing
methodologies for clarifying unknown phenomena, and deepening our understanding of nature by developing
logical trains of thought
• Those who are able to tackle new research targets and explore new frontiers on the basis of original ideas free
from previously established ways of thinking
• Those who aspire to play an active role internationally at the forefront of science by taking full advantage of the
expertise and advanced research abilities gained in their graduate school studies

Purpose of Education for Research
The purpose of research training in the Graduate School of Medicine is to promote advanced research
that will contribute to breakthroughs in the understanding of the mechanisms of life, to improve health,
to contribute to the struggle against disease, and to foster international leaders in all fields of medicine
who are outstanding scholars and highly creative researchers.
Admission Policy
1. Consistent with the aims described above, for its PhD programs in the Graduate School of Medicine,
the University of Tokyo seeks students who have the potential to become international leaders in all
fields of medicine through outstanding scholarship and advanced and highly creative research. We
seek those who will, as described above, contribute to the understanding of the mechanisms of life,
to preventing, curing, and recovering from disease, and to promoting and supporting healthy
human development.
2. Successful applicants will meet the following criteria.
- Successful applicants will, having a strong foundation of basic knowledge in medicine, nursing,
and health sciences, be able to do creative research aimed at solving problems in health sciences
and nursing for the purposes of advancing our understanding of the mechanisms of life; preventing,
curing, and recovering from disease; and promoting and supporting healthy human development.
- Successful applicants will be able to clear a path into the future of medicine, nursing, and health
sciences through clear, logical analysis and original thinking, unfettered by conventional concepts.
- Successful applicants will be able and motivated to become active international leaders in their
chosen field, on the basis of advanced knowledge and research skills acquired in graduate school.
- Successful applicants will have basic knowledge in medicine and health sciences and related
fields, and the ability to communicate in English as needed in lectures and seminars.
Each program in the Graduate School of Medicine seeks applicants who have the background
needed for that purpose to be fulfilled.
The aim of the Doctoral program in the School of International Health / Global Health Sciences is to
train international leaders who will pursue advanced research contributing to the understanding of
the mechanisms of life, the prevention and cure of disease, and the promotion of health through
outstanding scholarship and advanced creative research in all areas of medical science.

Graduate School of Medicine, the University of Tokyo

Purpose of Education for Research
The purpose of research training in the Graduate School of Medicine is to promote advanced research that
will contribute to breakthroughs in the understanding of the mechanisms of life, to improve health, to contribute
to the struggle against disease, and to foster international leaders in all fields of medicine who are outstanding
scholars and highly creative researchers.
Admission Policy
Consistent with the aims described above, for its PhD programs in the Graduate School of Medicine, the
University of Tokyo seeks students who have the potential to become international leaders in all fields of
medicine through outstanding scholarship and advanced and highly creative research. We seek those who
will, as described above, contribute to the understanding of the mechanisms of life, to preventing, curing, and
recovering from disease, and to promoting and supporting healthy human development.
1. Eligibility criteria
(1) Persons who have graduated or are expected to graduate from a program to study medicine or dentistry
at a Japanese university by 31 March 2027. (Note 1)
(2) Persons who have graduated or are expected to graduate from a six-year program to study pharmacy
or veterinary medicine at a Japanese university by 31 March 2027. (Note 1)
(3) Persons who have completed or are expected to complete 18 years of overseas curricular education
(ending with a program in medicine, dentistry, pharmacy or veterinary medicine) by 31 March 2027.
(Note 2)

a Bachelor's degree in medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, or veterinary medicine provided they meet
the conditions related to the restrictions described in (7) or (8) below, and after undergoing individual
eligibility review to determine whether their academic ability is equivalent or superior to that of
graduates of a program of study in medicine, etc., at a Japanese university, may be granted eligibility
to take the entrance examination -- provided that, before applying, they have undergone the
[individual] eligibility review whose application procedure requirements are described in Note 6 and
Note 7 below.
(4) Persons who have been conferred or are expected to be conferred a degree equivalent to that of a
bachelor's degree from a foreign university or other foreign institution of higher education (restricted to
those whose comprehensive status of education and research activities has been assessed by the
government of the said foreign country or a related organization) by March 31, 2027, having completed

Nota bene: Persons on whom degrees have been conferred (or are expected to be conferred) by a

(5) Persons who have graduated or are expected to graduate from an educational institution designated by

and are recognized by the Graduate School of Medicine based on an eligibility review as having
academic ability equivalent to or surpassing that of a person who has completed a program in medicine,
etc. (Note 5) at a Japanese university. (Note 1) (Note 6)
Nota bene: This clause pertains to persons with documented evidence of two or more years'
employment doing research at a university or a research institute etc.
(8) Persons who are recognized by the Graduate School of Medicine based on an individual eligibility review

are aged 24 years or older on 31 March 2027. (Note 7)
Nota bene: This clause pertains to persons on whom a Master's degree has been conferred by a foreign
university; note that, before applying for admission, applicants must undergo an individual eligibility
review (Note 7).
Note also that applicants' Bachelor's degree may have been conferred irrespectively either by a
Japanese or by a foreign university.
Nota bene: Clause (7) above, not this clause, pertains to persons with documented evidence of two or
more years' employment doing research at a university or a research institute etc.

Note 2: Eligibilities (3) and (4) above include the case of having completed a distance education program provided by a

Note 3: “Persons who have graduated or are expected to graduate from an educational institution designated by the

by the old University Ordinance and who have graduated from either of these faculties.
② Persons who have graduated from the National Defense Medical College under the Act for Establishment of
the Ministry of Defense.
③ Persons who have completed a program of a foreign university (restricted to those who have competed 18
years of bachelor’s education in the school education system of said foreign country, ending with a program
either in veterinary medicine, medicine, pharmacy or dentistry) while in Japan, the said program having been
designated separately by the Minister for Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology and belonging
to an educational institution under the school education system of the said foreign country.

Note 4: “Persons who have completed a master’s program, etc. at a Japanese university” as per eligibility criterion (6)

② Persons who have been enrolled for at least two years in a doctoral program which is not separated into
former and latter parts, and have obtained at least 30 academic credits in addition to the required research

Nota bene: Applicants are considered eligible if either ① or ② above pertain; prior review of their entrance

① A program to study medicine
② A program to study dentistry
③ A program to study pharmacy whose main purpose is to cultivate practical clinical ability
④ A program to study veterinary medicine

Persons who are deemed by the Graduate School of Medicine on the basis of an eligibility review to have

shall be permitted to take the entrance exam upon application.
Eligibility review result will be notified by Email around late June, 2026.
