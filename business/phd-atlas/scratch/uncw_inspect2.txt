Scholarships & Fellowships
The list below represents scholarships administered by the Graduate School, which are awarded on a competitive basis each year. To apply, please use the common scholarship application provided by the Office of Scholarships and Financial Aid. If you have problems with the application, please contact scholarships@uncw.edu. Please note that there may be additional scholarships offered by your program, please reach out to your program coordinator to learn about those opportunities.
Scholarship Applications
Applications are accepted until June 1 of each year. If you have problems with the application, please contact us. Please note that there may be additional scholarships offered by your program, please reach out to your program coordinator to learn about those opportunities.

Scholarships and Fellowships
Dr. Ralph W. Brauer Fellowship
The Dr. Ralph W. Brauer Fellowship was created through the estate of Dr. Brauer, a long tenured professor at UNCW. It is intended to provide financial support to one or more graduate students to assist with tuition and fees, books, publications, or research travel as determined by the dean of the graduate school. A committee appointed by the dean recommends recipients and the amount to be awarded.
Ahuja Academy of Water Quality Fellowship
The Ahuja Academy of Water Quality Fellowship was established by Dr. Satinder "Sut" Ahuja in recognition of his life-long career in the pharmaceutical industry and his desire to improve quality of life. This fellowship will be used to assist graduate students engaged in research related to water quality.
The Graduate School receives nominations for this fellowship from departments. Students interested in this award should notify their department.
Graduate School's Awards for New Scholars
A limited number of scholarships are available to students entering any of UNCW's graduate programs for the first time. Students do not apply for these scholarships. Instead, nominations originate with the departments, which submit their recommendations to the dean of the Graduate School.
Criteria include evidence of exceptional scholarship, normally strong GRE scores (or their equivalent) and an excellent undergraduate GPA.
Got-Em-On Live Bait Club Graduate Fellowship
The Got-Em-On Live Bait Club Graduate Fellowship is intended to provide financial support for a student who has been accepted into the graduate program, who will study fish biology and whose interest and research focus on the issues affecting the fish population in the waters of Southeastern North Carolina.
The Graduate School receives nominations for this fellowship from departments. Students interested in this award should notify their department.
Jane Logan Lackey Fellowship
The Jane Logan Lackey Fellowship is allocated to promote diversity within the Graduate School. An undergraduate GPA of at least 3.25 in the applicant's major is required.
Sylvia and B. D. Schwartz Graduate Fellowship
The Sylvia and B. D. Schwartz Graduate Fellowship may be awarded to any graduate student enrolled in the University of North Carolina Wilmington. The recipients are determined by a select committee appointed by the Dean of the Graduate School.
Lacy C. and Doris L. Sidbury Fellowship
The Lacy C. and Doris L. Sidbury Graduate Fellowship was established by Gwendolyn S. Solomon as a memorial to her parents. It is considered both a merit and need-based fellowship, subject to the criteria used by both the UNCW Graduate School and the Financial Aid and Veterans Services Office.
Additional Scholarships and Fellowships
There are several other scholarship opportunities offered to you as a UNCW graduate student.

Graduate Assistantships
Earn while you learn! Graduate students may work as salaried or hourly employees through assistantships or other on campus employment opportunities to receive a monthly paycheck and/or tuition waiver.
Graduate Teaching Assistant Benefits:
Students working as Graduate Teaching Assistants who are identified and working as the instructor of
record teaching at least one course may be eligible for a GTA parking permit that will allow them to park in non-gated Faculty/Staff parking areas. These GTA permits will be available on a yearly basis.
Note: A TA parking permit cost is equal to the cost of a Zone 1 parking permit. Students that are eligible for a TA parking permit and currently have a non-TA parking permit can upgrade to a TA parking permit. Students that upgrade their current permit to a TA permit will be required to pay the cost difference between the permits.
Graduate students must be teaching a course/lab. (i.e. graduate students who are the sole teaching authority in a full lecture class). Students will initially be assigned to Zone 1 but are eligible to upgrade to a TA permit.
GA's and RA's: Graduate students with GAs, RAs or other campus employment are not eligible for TA parking.
Graduate Teaching Assistant Awards
Four (4) Graduate Teaching Awards ($250 each) will be awarded by the Graduate School in recognition of outstanding teaching.
GRADUATE RESEARCH ASSISTANT BENEFITS:
Graduate Research Assistants receive tuition support in addition to their monthly stipend. Students enrolled in master's programs will receive the equivalent of the NC Resident (In-State) portion of their tuition, while students enrolled in doctoral programs will receive both the NC Resident and NC Non-Resident portions of their tuition.

Science Olympiad
Gamma Sigma Epsilon is a national chemistry honor society founded in 1919 at Davidson College.
The original fraternity was created to promote academic excellence and undergraduate research scholarship in chemistry. In 1931 the fraternity went co-ed and became an honor society.
Today there are over seventy active Gamma Sigma Epsilon chapters throughout the United States. The Society is governed by a National Executive Council of elected officers and meets in biennial conventions for the purpose of governance, fellowship, and scientific exchange.
The Chemistry Club is a club focused on community outreach and exposing the general public to information involving chemistry and increasing awareness of chemistry's impact on everyday life.
Science Olympiad is a targeted opportunity to serve and recruit interested students in science, technology, engineering and mathematics.
Did your middle school or high school compete in Science Olympiad? If so, then you know what we mean! If not, then it is time to find out why Science Olympiad is the most important science competition around!
Find more information about Science Olympiad including upcoming middle school and high school events.

Tuition & Fees
Graduate Tuition
Tuition and Fees are billed by the credit hour based on the student’s program campus code, student level, and residency. Tuition and Fees are subject to change pending legislative approval.
Certain graduate professional programs carry a differential tuition, or “premium,” in addition to the regular graduate tuition. This increased tuition charge is used to maintain & enhance the excellence of the program while assuring access to them.
For an estimated cost of attendance per academic year, please visit the Estimated Cost of Attendance.
Find the 2026-2027 per-credit-hour tuition rates for graduate students at UNCW in the tables below.
